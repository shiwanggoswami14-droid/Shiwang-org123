Your Safety & Privacy

Trust & Transparency

ScamShield AI assists users in identifying potential scam indicators. Results are guidance, not guarantees, and should be checked against trusted official sources.

  • On-Demand Assistance: ScamShield AI checks only content you choose to submit. It does not monitor private messages, WhatsApp or calls in the background.
  • No Absolute Guarantees: Findings can help surface warning signs, but no result can guarantee that content is safe or fraudulent.
  • Client-Side Privacy: Analysis is performed in your browser. Your private messages are not sent to a remote marketing database.

About ScamShield AI

Think Before You Trust

Our Mission

ScamShield AI is designed to protect everyday people—including students, parents, working professionals, and seniors—from digital extortion, fake job scams, electricity bill fraud, and banking phishing. We believe cybersecurity should be simple, welcoming, and clear.

How It Works

ScamShield AI uses a deterministic security heuristics engine to inspect language coercion patterns (such as urgent deadlines, threats of arrest, or utility disconnection), unverified telephone numbers, and deceptive website addresses.

Our Limitations (Transparency First)

ScamShield AI does not monitor your WhatsApp, SMS, or phone calls in the background. It only analyses the specific content you choose to submit. Risk scores reflect heuristic proximity to known fraud patterns and should never replace personal skepticism or direct verification with official organizations.
